XLSX tools - third-party notices

The XLSX tools contain no code copied from other projects. The compression and
decompression modules (XLSX01R, XLSX02R) are written in ILE RPG and follow
published algorithms of zlib and of puff, by Jean-loup Gailly and Mark Adler:

- XLSX01R: the matching decisions of zlib's deflate_slow (lazy matching) at
  levels 5-9, and the CRC-32 computed by slicing on big-endian words as in
  zlib's crc32_big;
- XLSX02R: the canonical Huffman decoding used by puff for codes longer than
  the 9-bit lookup table.
